## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the difference between regular bubble wrap and anti-static bubble wrap?

Both can provide cushioning, but anti-static bubble wrap is designed for applications where protection against static buildup or discharge is also a consideration.

### Is anti-static bubble wrap suitable for electronics?

Yes. It is commonly used for sensitive electronic components because it combines cushioning with anti-static protection.

### Can I use it for heavier products?

The appropriate bubble thickness depends on the product's weight, shape, fragility, and handling conditions. Heavier or sharper products may require a more substantial cushioning solution.

### Can the rolls be customized?

Yes. BlueRose Packaging states that anti-static bubble rolls can be customized in different lengths and thicknesses, with options such as custom widths and perforations.

### Can bubble rolls be used inside corrugated boxes?

Yes. Bubble cushioning can be used as an internal protective layer before placing the wrapped product inside a corrugated shipping carton.

### Should anti-static bubble wrap be the only protection for sensitive electronics?

Not necessarily. The packaging should be selected according to the product's sensitivity, weight, fragility, shipping conditions, and ESD requirements. Additional foam, cartons, partitions, or other protective materials may be appropriate.
